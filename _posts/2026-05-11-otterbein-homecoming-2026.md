# Kings Fraternity Homecoming 2026: A Celebration of Brotherhood and Tradition

This fall, the Kings Fraternity family is coming together for an unforgettable Homecoming celebration at Otterbein University. Whether you graduated decades ago or recently walked across the stage, this is your invitation to reconnect with the brotherhood and experience the vibrant culture of Kings Fraternity.

## What to Expect

### A Gathering at the House
We're opening our doors for an intimate homecoming lunch right here at the Kings Fraternity house. It's the perfect opportunity to catch up with old friends, meet the current active chapter, and relive the memories that made your time at Otterbein special. Come hungry and ready to share stories!

### Annual Alumni Chapter Meeting
During Homecoming weekend, we'll be hosting our annual alumni chapter meeting. This is an important gathering where we discuss the fraternity's direction, celebrate achievements from the past year, and hear updates from our undergraduate brothers. Whether you're an active member of our alumni network or just want to catch up on what's happening, we encourage you to attend.

### A Walk Down Memory Lane
One of the highlights of Homecoming is the opportunity to reconnect with the active chapter and see how the house has evolved over the years. We'll take a walk through campus and the chapter house, reminiscing about traditions, inside jokes, and the moments that defined our fraternity experience. Current undergraduates will be excited to meet their predecessors and hear firsthand the legacy they're carrying forward.

## Why Attend?

**For the Brotherhood:** Kings Fraternity is more than just a college organization—it's a lifelong bond. Homecoming is a chance to strengthen those connections and prove that distance and time can't diminish the brotherhood we share.

**For the Memories:** Whether it's your first Homecoming or your tenth, you'll find yourself laughing about shared experiences and making new memories with both old and new brothers.

**For Continuity:** By participating in Homecoming, you're showing the active chapter that the values and traditions of Kings Fraternity endure. You're part of a legacy that extends far beyond your four years.

## Mark Your Calendars

Details about specific dates and times for our Homecoming events will be shared soon. In the meantime, clear your fall schedule and prepare for a weekend of food, fellowship, and fraternity pride.

Stay tuned for registration information and event updates!

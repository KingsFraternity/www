Kings Fraternity is a fraternity on the Otterbein University campus, founded in **1948**. Built on the pillars of brotherhood, leadership, and service, we have proudly called Westerville, Ohio home for over 75 years.

**Greek Letters:** Λ Γ Ε (Lambda Gamma Epsilon)

**Colors:** Maroon and Gold

**Fraternity House:** 94 West Park St., Westerville, OH

We welcome members who share our values and commitment to making a positive impact.

Feel free to reach out to us on [GitHub](https://github.com/KingsFraternity).
